# Run your shop's marketing from one place

**social.** keeps the whole marketing loop in one app: what you plan to publish, what you
spend, what it brought back, and what to do next.

It replaces the usual mess of a spreadsheet for spend, a note for content ideas, and a
"where did this order come from?" guess at the end of the month.

## The whole loop in four steps

1. **Plan a campaign** and give it a short, unique *UTM campaign* name.
2. **Tag every link** you post with that name, so visits and orders can be traced back.
3. **Record results** — either import a CSV from your ad platform, or let Google Analytics
   pull them in automatically.
4. **Read the Dashboard** to see which campaign earned more than it cost, then act on the
   **Suggestions**.

!!! tip "New here?"

## What you need before you start

| You need | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A login for the app | Accounts are created for you — there is no public sign-up. See [Getting started](guide/getting-started.md). |
| A list of the campaigns you are running | Even a rough one. Names can be changed later. |
| A way to get daily numbers | Either a CSV export from your ad platform, or a connected Google Analytics 4 property. |
